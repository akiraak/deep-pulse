Vibe coding has accelerated application development across organizations. Non-technical employees can now build functional tools in hours. This advancement has expanded innovation but also introduced significant cybersecurity concerns, said Ami Luttwak, co-founder and CTO of Wiz.

**See Also:** [Why Every AI Decision Begins With DNS](https://www.govinfosecurity.com/every-ai-decision-begins-dns-a-31559?rf=RAM_SeeAlso)

\"When someone who is non-technical creates this amazing application, many times they don\'t think about security and they don\'t even know what\'s inside the application because they didn\'t even create it on their own,\" Luttwak said.

He warned that many of these applications can bypass authentication and expose sensitive data, creating new risks for organizations. \"What happens is that many times when people create those applications, they expose them to the internet, and that\'s the problem. As long as it runs on your own computer, it\'s risky, but at least it\'s not dangerous,\" he said.

In this video interview with Information Security Media Group at [RSAC Conference 2026](/rsac-conference#p-1), Luttwak also discussed:

-   How artificial intelligence can be used in uncovering defensive cybersecurity vulnerabilities;
-   The rise of financially motivated attackers using advanced AI tools;
-   How the Google Cloud-Wiz integration is strengthening threat intelligence and AI defenses.

Luttwak drives the strategic vision for Wiz, leading the oversight of all security research and milestone discoveries in AI and the cloud. He has more than 15 years of experience in cloud security innovation. Before co-founding Wiz, he was CTO for Israel R&D at Microsoft.
