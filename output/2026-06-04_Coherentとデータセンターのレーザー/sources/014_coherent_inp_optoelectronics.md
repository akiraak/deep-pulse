# InP Optoelectronics

Use these InP-based lasers, devices, and photodiode products, for high-speed O- C- and L-band fiber optical communications.

### High-Power 100 mW DFB Laser Diode Chips

Get 100 mW of uncooled output power and 300 mW of output power when cooled, to enable 100 Gbps and 200 Gbps per lane, respectively, for cutting-edge O-band transceivers.

Use these 13XX nm laser diode chips in high-speed uncooled transceivers based on NRZ or PAM4 (four-level) modulation, available at all four O-band CWDM wavelengths.

Use these Fabry-Perot (FP) and distributed feedback (DFB) laser diode chips to build optical transceivers and other components needing a few mW of 13XX nm laser output.

Optimal transmitter devices for optical transceivers employing 56 GBd and 112 GBd PAM4 modulation.

### Edge-illuminated Monitor Photodiode (EMPD) Chips

Use these self-hermetic devices for channel monitoring anywhere across the 1270-1620 nm wavelength range with a typical responsivity of 0.8 A/W at 1310 nm.

### 1X4 Photodiode Arrays for NRZ or PAM4

Use these 13XX nm photodiode array chips in pluggable transceivers configured for (28 Gbps) NRZ or (56 Gbps) PAM4, and benefit from their low dark current and high reliability.
