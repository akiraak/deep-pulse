[Stephen King](/wiki/Stephen_King "Stephen King") is an [American author](/wiki/American_literature "American literature") of contemporary horror, thriller, science fiction, and fantasy. His books have sold more than 400 million copies,^[[\[]{.cite-bracket}1[\]]{.cite-bracket}](#cite_note-1)\[*[[needs\ update]{title="Very outdated numbers as of 2006 (August 2023)"}](/wiki/Wikipedia:Manual_of_Style/Dates_and_numbers#Chronological_items "Wikipedia:Manual of Style/Dates and numbers")*\]^ and many of them have been [adapted into feature films, television movies, and comic books](/wiki/List_of_adaptations_of_works_by_Stephen_King "List of adaptations of works by Stephen King"). King has published 67 novels/novellas, including seven under the pen name [Richard Bachman](/wiki/Richard_Bachman "Richard Bachman"), and five nonfiction books. He has written [more than 200 short stories](/wiki/Stephen_King_short_fiction_bibliography "Stephen King short fiction bibliography"), most of which have been compiled in book collections. Many of his works are set in his home state of [Maine](/wiki/Maine "Maine").
